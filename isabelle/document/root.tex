\title{NumpyCpp Verification with Isabelle/HOL}
\author{Sergio Randriamihoatra / Muse Spark}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
Formal verification of \texttt{numpy-cpp} differential and lattice kernels
using Isabelle/HOL. Theories \texttt{Dual\_Verification}, \texttt{Differential\_Verification},
\texttt{Lattice\_Verification} correspond to \texttt{include/np/differential.hpp}
and \texttt{include/np/lattice.hpp} and prove key properties: Dual forward AD
correctness, exterior derivative nilpotency, symbolic differentiation, lattice
meet/join laws, dual involution, LLL invariants, and factory correctness.
\end{abstract}
